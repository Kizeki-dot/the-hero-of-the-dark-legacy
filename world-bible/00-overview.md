# World Overview

## 🌍 World Setting
A vast world where multiple species coexist:
- Humans
- Elves
- Dwarves
- Other unknown races

The world is protected by an ancient Great Magic Shield created during the Hero Age.

## ⚔️ Current Era
Known as the Hero Age:
- Heroes are born naturally
- Only Heroes can use Hero Magic
- Heroes awaken abilities at ages 6–8
- Hero Academy begins at age 10

## 💀 Hidden Truth
Dark Magic and Hero Magic share a forgotten ancient origin.

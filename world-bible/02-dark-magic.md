# Dark Magic

## 🌑 Nature of Dark Magic
Dark Magic is a primal force that:
- Consumes mana
- Adapts by devouring energy
- Corrupts unstable minds

## 💀 Dark Monsters
Dark Monsters are not a separate species.

They are:
- Ancient beings corrupted by Dark Magic
- Or beings that lost control of mana consumption

## ⚠️ Effect on User
Prolonged use can lead to:
- Mental corruption
- Identity loss
- Transformation into a monster-like state

## 👁️ Special Case
MC is the only known human capable of using Dark Magic.

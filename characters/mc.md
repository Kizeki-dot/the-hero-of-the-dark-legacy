# Main Character

## 🧒 Profile
- Son of two legendary heroes
- Kind-hearted and socially skilled
- Loved by everyone before the incident

## 💀 Event
- Attacked by an ancient dark monster
- Saved by a paladin but nearly died
- Awakened Dark Magic instead of Hero Magic

## ⚠️ Status
- Feared by society
- Seen as unnatural existence
- Only human capable of Dark Magic

## 🎯 Core Conflict
- Risk of being consumed by Dark Magic
- Struggles between humanity and corruption
